8. Age

Rolff is only for people aged 18 or older. You confirm this when you create an account; the time is stored with your account. We do not collect your date of birth.

9. Security

HTTPS in transit. Rolff does not offer end-to-end encryption. A local browser copy is encrypted with a key that stays on the device.

10. Local storage (no tracking cookies)

Rolff uses no advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser stores: your login session (Supabase), language, drafts of messages you have not sent yet, and whether you dismissed the install tip. A local copy of conversations is stored encrypted in the browser database (IndexedDB). When you log out, the login session and the local copy are removed; language and drafts stay in the browser. If you delete your account, everything Rolff stored in the browser is removed.

11. Blocked topics

Your optional meaning-based criteria are private from other participants. Recipient criteria are sent to the configured AI service with the message and short context excerpts (and extracted attachment text). AI can make mistakes; uncertain meaning requires clarification. Criteria are stored with your account and included in your own data export, never disclosed to senders in rejection messages. An EU endpoint does not in itself mean that processing takes place only in the EU. There is no agreement on EU data residency or zero data retention (ZDR). Without ZDR, content may be retained for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring and is not used for training.
